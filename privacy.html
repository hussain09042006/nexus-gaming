Privacy & disclosure.

This page describes storage and links used by the current static website.

Information stored in this browser

Favourites, wishlist, recently viewed games, the comparison selection (up to four games), the preferred library view, and personal list status, ratings, and notes are stored in this browser's local storage on this device. Personal data does not sync to an account, browser, or other device. Export a JSON backup on My Games to move or preserve your data; importing a valid backup merges it with data already in this browser. If malformed personal-list data is encountered, it is kept as a recovery value rather than silently discarded during a valid import. You can also remove individual items on My Games or clear this site's browser storage.

Personal ratings and notes remain private to this browser and are not displayed as public reviews or game ratings. If browser storage is unavailable, saving may fail and the site will announce the problem. Malformed stored values are left unchanged when read.

External artwork and retailer websites

Game artwork is requested from the external image host referenced by the catalog. If you follow a retailer link, that retailer's site may receive a request from your browser and its own privacy policy and practices apply. Nexus Gaming does not control those external services.

The site does not currently use analytics or outbound-click tracking. No click data or personal library data is sent to a service by this website.

Affiliate disclosure

Nexus Gaming may earn a commission from eligible purchases made through clearly identified, approved affiliate links. Affiliate links will only be displayed after the destination and offer details are verified and the required program approval is granted. No affiliate offer is currently configured; Green Man Gaming approval for this site's ad space is pending.

Prices, currency, and availability are shown only when supplied as verified offer data and may change at the retailer. Check the retailer's page before purchasing.

Changes

This notice should be reviewed whenever the site's storage or external integrations change.
